## Reflection on Speaker (Dr. Snehesh Shrestha)
I thought Dr. Shrestha gave a very compelling lecture on the merits and drawbacks of standards in engineering; it was well organized, he provided relevant and easy-to-understand examples, 
and he efficiently conveyed the importance of lectures in the engineering field. I enjoyed his use of humor throughout the lecture (e.g. the "Independence Day" clip and mems about the tendency 
of standards to multiply), as well as his overall coverage of the topic. I learned a lot about *who* exactly writes standards, *why* they are typically voluntary, and I enjoyed learning about 
some of the cool examples throughout history such as the standardization of music and time. All in all, it definitely made me appreciate standards a lot more, and it got me thinking about 
the USB Standard and how that has succeeded, failed, and changed over the years (like the USB-C standard we are using for the ESP32).
